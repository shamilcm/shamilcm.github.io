I am a Staff Machine Learning Researcher at Zoom, where I lead multilingual LLM development and have taken multilingual LLMs from research to production at scale. Before Zoom, I was a machine translation research scientist at Rakuten. I have a PhD in natural language processing (NLP) from National University of Singapore (NUS), and I was a recipient of the ISEP Scholarship (formerly NGS scholarship).

My areas of expertise include multilingual LLM post-training, multilinguality evaluation, machine translation, and speech translation.
